# King-Fahd-National-Museum
Architectural tensile fabric roof structure for King Fahd National Museum in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
King Fahd National Museum – Tensile Fabric Roof
Architectural Design Meets Performance

The King Fahd National Museum in Riyadh is one of Saudi Arabia’s prominent cultural landmarks, where architecture and identity come together to create a distinctive visitor experience.

As part of the project, C.Force contributed to the development and execution of a tensile fabric roof structure, combining architectural aesthetics with functional performance.

A Lightweight Architectural Solution

Tensile membrane structures offer an efficient solution for creating large-span architectural spaces while maintaining a lightweight and visually distinctive design.

For the King Fahd National Museum, the fabric roof adds an elegant architectural element while providing effective shading and protection for the designated space.
